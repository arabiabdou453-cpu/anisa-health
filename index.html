          The Healthcare Crisis
        

        
The $52 Billion Post-Discharge Chasm

        

          Why hospital readmissions continue to burden healthcare systems, and why conventional methods fail.
        

      


      

        

          
80%

          
The Literacy Gap

          

            Clinical discharge paperwork is written at a university reading level with dense abbreviations. Over 80% of patients forget verbal guidance within 2 hours of leaving the hospital doors.
          

        


        

          
1 in 5

          
Readmitted in 30 Days

          

            Nearly 20% of discharged patients return to the emergency department within 30 days due to mismanaged medications or unmonitored symptoms that could have been prevented with early triage.
          

        


        

          
85%

          
The App Download Barrier

          

            Elderly patients and vulnerable populations do not download portal apps or remember complex passwords. Anisa operates directly inside the mobile web browser via simple SMS or WhatsApp links.
          

        

      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        

          Core Technology
        

        
Built on Human-First Principles

        

          Anisa pairs clinical safety guardrails with compassionate, patient-centered communication.
        

      


      

        

          

            
          

          

            
Automated Clinical Ingestion

            
Direct integration with Epic, Cerner, and hospital EHRs. Anisa extracts verified physician discharge summaries without adding manual clicks to overburdened nurses.

          

        


        

          

            
          

          

            
Empathy-Tuned Plain Language

            
Translates complex pharmacological regimens into 6th-grade reading level conversational narratives. Supports audio playback for patients with visual or literacy barriers.

          

        


        

          

            
          

          

            
Teach-Back Comprehension Checks

            
Rather than asking "Do you understand?", Anisa uses evidence-based teach-back questions to confirm patients know exactly which medications to take and which to stop.

          

        


        

          

            
          

          

            
Proactive Red-Flag Triage

            
Automated daily conversational check-ins detect early warning signs (weight spikes, wound redness, fever) and immediately escalate to the clinic care team before readmission.

          Clinically Proven Foundation
        

        
Grounded in Boston University's Project RED

        

          Project RED (Re-Engineered Discharge) is recognized by the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ) and NIH as the gold standard for reducing preventable readmissions. Anisa automates all 12 key components.
        


        

          

            
STEP 01 — MED RECONCILIATION

            
Clear Medicine Action Plan

            
Explicitly compares pre-hospital pills with new discharge medications, highlighting discontinued drugs.

          


          

            
STEP 02 — PLAIN LANGUAGE

            
Teach-Back Confirmation

            
Confirms patient comprehension on dosage, frequency, and purpose using interactive conversational loops.

          


          

            
STEP 03 — DANGER SIGNALS

            
Actionable What-If Plan

            
Empowers patients to recognize warning symptoms and gives clear, unambiguous guidance on who to call.
